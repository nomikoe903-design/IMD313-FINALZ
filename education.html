Where it all began

Three chapters of education.

My academic journey, from primary school to university.

2013–2018 · Primary education

SK Bumiputera

I began my education at SK Bumiputera, where I served as a school prefect from Year 4 until Year 6. I also participated in an action song competition at district level. These activities became memorable parts of my primary school experience.

2019–2023 · Secondary education

SMK Lundu

At SMK Lundu, I studied Economics, Literature and Geography. I continued serving as a school prefect from Form 1 to Form 5 and received the Excellent Student Award in Forms 4 and 5.

I also joined a STEM competition with a science project. One of my proudest memories was winning first place in a state-level video competition.

2024–Present · Higher education

Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) Sarawak

Campus: Samarahan  ·  Programme: CDIM110 — Diploma in Information Management

I started studying at UiTM in 2024 after receiving an offer for the Information Management programme through UPU. What I value most about university life is meeting friends who are always there to help and support one another.

I earned the Dean’s List Award in Semesters 1, 2 and 4. Another meaningful experience was delivering a talk promoting UiTM alongside my close friends during a community service programme at SMK Tebedu.
